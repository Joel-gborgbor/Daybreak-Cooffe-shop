A neighbourhood café with a simple idea

We opened Daybreak to make a good cup of coffee part of an ordinary morning. We buy from small farms, roast in small batches and bake our pastries before we open the doors.

Stay for a while, bring a book, or take your coffee to go. Either way, you are welcome here.
